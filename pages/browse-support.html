Flexible Ways to Help

How You Can Support Families in Your Area

Whether you love home cooking or live across the country, there are multiple ways to make a meaningful difference.

Home Cooking

Home-Cooked Meals

Prepare a warm, comforting dinner tailored to recipient dietary preferences and drop it off during the requested window.

Local Restaurants

Takeout Delivery

Short on time? Order fresh meals from local restaurants or favorite takeout spots delivered directly to their doorstep.

Long Distance

Digital Gift Cards

Participating from afar? Send grocery or food delivery e-gift cards so the family can order what they need, when they need it.

Pantry Care

Snacks & Essentials

Drop off fresh fruit, breakfast items, beverages, or kid-friendly snacks to keep the family pantry stocked during stressful times.
