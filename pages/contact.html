Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting a meal train, coordinating volunteers, and supporting families.

How much does it cost to organize or join a meal train?

MealTrain Direct is 100% free for coordinators, families, and community supporters. There are no hidden fees or subscription costs to set up or manage a schedule.

How do I share the meal train with friends and family?

Once you create a schedule, you will receive a unique, private link. You can easily copy and share this link via email, text message, WhatsApp, or social media with your trusted group.

What if the recipient family has severe food allergies?

Coordinators can list all dietary restrictions, food dislikes, household size, and drop-off guidelines at the top of the schedule. Volunteers see these notes before choosing a date.

Can long-distance friends contribute if they can't cook a meal?

Yes! Long-distance supporters can send digital food gift cards or arrange takeout delivery from local restaurants directly through the recipient's schedule link.

How are drop-off times and instructions managed?

Coordinators specify preferred delivery windows (e.g., between 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM), porch cooler locations, and text update rules so families are never disturbed.
